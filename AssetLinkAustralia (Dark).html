FAQ

Common Questions. Direct Answers.

Everything you need to make a confident decision.

No jargon. No obligation. Just straight answers.

What types of property do you work with?

We work across commercial office, retail, industrial, mixed-use, strata, and freehold properties. Our clients range from single-asset owners to multi-property institutional portfolios across Australia.

Do you work with tenants or only owners?

Both. We represent commercial tenants in lease negotiations, workplace strategy, and cost reduction — and we represent owners in asset management, leasing, and compliance. We never act for both sides in the same transaction.

How quickly can you identify savings or improvements?

Most clients see an initial opportunity assessment within 2–4 weeks of engagement. Operational improvements typically begin within 30–60 days. Strategic outcomes are measured over 6–24 months depending on scope.

What does it cost to engage Asset Link Australia?

Fees vary by engagement type and scope. We offer fixed-fee advisory engagements, performance-based structures, and retained management agreements. Book a consultation to discuss what's right for your situation.

How is Asset Link Australia different from a traditional property manager?

Traditional property managers focus on day-to-day operations. We integrate strategic advisory with operational delivery — meaning we don't just manage your building, we actively improve its performance, value, and competitiveness.

Can you help with ESG compliance and sustainability?

Yes. We conduct ESG audits, develop sustainability roadmaps, manage green retrofit projects, and support certification processes. Non-compliant buildings increasingly face higher vacancy and lower valuations — we help you get ahead of that.

Do you work outside of major cities?

Yes. We work across metropolitan and regional markets in Australia. Our advisory capability is location-agnostic, and our operational delivery network covers major centres and key regional hubs.

What happens in the first consultation?

We listen first. We'll ask about your property, your goals, and your current challenges. We'll share relevant market context and initial observations. There's no obligation and no sales pitch — just a direct conversation about whether we can help.
